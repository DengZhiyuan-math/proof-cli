burger-integral-formula
	
Let $(\pi,\mathcal{H})$ be a unitary representation of $SL_2(\mathbb{R})$ (no nonzero invariant vectors), $f\in\mathcal{H}^\infty$, $\mathcal{Y}\ge1$, $T>0$, $h(t)=h_\infty(t)$, $a(y)=\mathrm{diag}(y^{1/2},y^{-1/2})$. Then $\frac1T\int_0^T\pi(h(t))f\,dt=\frac1T\int_0^T\pi(h(t)a(\mathcal{Y}))F_{\mathcal{Y}}f\,dt-\frac{1}{2T}\int_0^T\pi(h(t)a(\mathcal{Y}))S_{\mathcal{Y}}\,d\pi(Y)f\,dt+\frac1T\int_0^{\mathcal{Y}}[1-\pi(h(T))]\pi(a(y))T_y\,d\pi(X_-)f\,dy$, the last integral converging in $\mathcal{H}$.

cites burgerhorocycle: Horocycle flow on geometrically finite surfaces — Marc Burger (1990) · key lemma (number not read); the form with F_Y, S_Y, T_y is the draft display (equationfromburger) · Duke Math. J. 61 (1990)

burger-operator-bounds
	
With $s=s(\xi)$ and $f_y,s_y,t_y$ as in the Burger operators: $\sup_{\xi\in Z^0_-\cup Z^{ct}}|f_y(\xi)|\le\frac{2+\log y}{2\sqrt y}$ and $\sup_{\xi\in Z^0_-\cup Z^{ct}}|s_y(\xi)|\le\frac{\log y}{\sqrt y}$ for $y\ge1$; $\sup_{\xi\in Z^0_-\cup Z^{ct}}|t_y(\xi)|\le\frac{2\log(y+10)}{\sqrt{y+1}}$ for $y\in(0,\mathcal{Y}]$. Consequently $F_y,S_y,T_y$ are bounded on $\mathcal{H}$ and on each $W_k(\mathcal{H})$, continuous on $\mathcal{H}^\infty$, and $y\mapsto T_y$ is continuous in operator norm on $W_k(\mathcal{H})$.

cites burgerhorocycle: Horocycle flow on geometrically finite surfaces — Marc Burger (1990) · bounds on f_y, s_y, t_y (number not read); the draft displays (fsboundofintertwiningoperator), (tboundofintertwiningoperator) · Duke Math. J. 61 (1990)

chh-tempered-bound
	
Let $L$ be $G_\infty$ or $G_p$ with maximal compact subgroup $K$ ($K_\infty$, resp. $K_p$). If $\pi$ is a tempered unitary representation of $L$ and $v,w$ are $K$-finite, then $|\langle\pi(g)v,w\rangle|\le(d_K(v)d_K(w))^{1/2}\|v\|\|w\|\,\Xi_L(g)$ for all $g\in L$.

emmv-uniform-decay
	
Let $\mathbf{G}$ be a simply connected semisimple group over a number field $F$ as in Theorem 4.1. There is $\kappa_2>0$ such that for every place $v$, all $K_v$-finite $f_1,f_2\in L^2(\mathbf{G}(F)\backslash\mathbf{G}(\mathbb{A}))$ with $\int f_1=\int f_2=0$ and all $g_v\in\mathbf{G}(F_v)$: $|\langle\pi_{g_v}f_1,f_2\rangle|\le\dim\langle K_v f_1\rangle^{1/2}\dim\langle K_v f_2\rangle^{1/2}\|f_1\|_2\|f_2\|_2\,\Xi_{G_v}(g_v)^{\kappa_2}$, where $K_v$ is a good maximal compact open subgroup of $\mathbf{G}(F_v)$ and $\Xi_{G_v}$ a Harish-Chandra spherical function; $\kappa_2$ is independent of $v$.

exceptional-cuspidal-finite-per-level

For every compact open subgroup $K<SL_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$, the set of $\xi\in Z^0_+$ with $\mathcal{H}_{p,\xi}^{K}\neq0$ (exceptional cuspidal $\xi$ whose $p$-adic factor has a nonzero $K$-fixed vector) is finite, and $\sum_{\xi\in Z^0_+}\dim\mathcal{H}_{p,\xi}^{K}<\infty$.

local-sobolev-inequality
	
Let $M$ be the unit tangent bundle of a finite-area hyperbolic surface (a 3-manifold, locally $SL_2(\mathbb{R})$). For $0<\epsilon$ below the injectivity radius at $y\in M$ there is $C(\epsilon)$ with $|u(y)|^2\le C(\epsilon)\int_{B(y,\epsilon)}|u|^2+|du|^2+|\Delta u|^2$ for all $u\in W^{2,2}$; in particular $W^{2,2}$ functions are continuous.

cites flaminio-forni2003: Invariant distributions and time averages for horocycle flows — Livio Flaminio, Giovanni Forni (2003) · classical local Sobolev embedding; locator not read · Duke Math. J. 119 (2003)

tree-spherical-asymptotics
	
On $G_p$: (a) the Harish-Chandra function satisfies $\Xi_p(a_n)=\frac{p^{-n}}{p+1}\big((2n+1)(p-1)+2\big)$ for $n\ge0$; (b) the spherical function of the complementary series $\gamma^s$, $s\in(0,1)$, satisfies $\varpi_{\gamma^s}(a_n)\ll\frac{p^{n(s-1)}}{1-p^{-s}}$.
