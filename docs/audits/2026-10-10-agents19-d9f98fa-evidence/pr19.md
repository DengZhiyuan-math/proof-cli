## What

- **Proof budget:** a node's proof budget (`[studio] budget` turns) covers Prover, Verifier, Typesetter and Numerics turns. It is a ceiling, not an allocation.
- **Structure allowance:** Decomposer turns are structure, counted as `structure_turns` on the node's entry and not in its proof turns. Per parent, there is one first split (an unsplit root) and one re-split after a parked Claim. A second re-split is refused (`resplit_refused`, noted once).
- **One Pursue total:**
  - `PursueBudget` holds the turns and the time limit, fixed at Start and shared by a project Pursue's Reader and each Theorem's Coordinator.
  - The default is `pursue_limits`: node budget × max(1, open nodes at Start) + 2 per open Theorem or Lemma (+1 for the Reader when a turn is due).
  - The researcher can pass their own `limits={"turns", "minutes"}`.
  - The total is never raised. A split's Claims, retries and re-splits spend what is left of it.
  - A run's cap is what is left of its node budget and of the total, once the running runs' caps are set aside. `AgentRun.start(minutes=)` caps the run's time by what is left of the time limit.
- **Failed launches:**
  - A run that ends `stuck` or `budget` before any turn spends no turn.
  - Three in a Start (the first attempt counted, not reset by a success) and the node is not tried again; its claim is given back and the reason is recorded in `launch_failed`.
  - A first split or a re-split whose launch failed is tried again as a split.
  - Each Start's end notes real turns (structure and proof) and failed launches separately.
- **At the end of the total or the time limit:** as in #18, nothing new starts, running runs end, and nodes are Parked or Interrupted. A parent whose re-split was due is Interrupted, with a reason saying the total prevented it. A project Pursue starts no further Theorem (`not-started`) and ends `budget`.
